Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers peanutbutterporkchop.com and its sites: www, visual and shop. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

We have no accounts, sign-ups or contact forms, so we don't ask you for personal information. The only data gathered when you visit comes from the services below.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see which pages get visited. It sets cookies and records things like pages viewed, referring site, device and browser type, and approximate location derived from your IP address. See Google's Privacy Policy, or block it with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Hosting and content delivery

The sites are served by Cloudflare, which processes request data such as your IP address and user agent to deliver pages and protect against abuse. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy. Some pages load code libraries from third-party CDNs, which also see your IP address when your browser requests those files.

Shop purchases

Checkout, payment and shipping are handled by Fourthwall. Anything you enter at checkout is collected and processed by Fourthwall under the Fourthwall Privacy Policy, not stored by us.

Your choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use the opt-out add-on above. The sites work fine without analytics cookies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions or requests: privacy@peanutbutterporkchop.com.
